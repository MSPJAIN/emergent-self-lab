# Contributing to Emergent Self Lab

Thanks for taking the time to look closely at this project.

You do not need to agree with the idea behind an experiment to make a useful contribution. Careful criticism is part of the work.

## Helpful contributions

You can help by:

- pointing out a hidden confound;
- suggesting a stronger control group;
- showing that a simpler system could produce the same behavior;
- identifying a measurement problem;
- reproducing a public result; or
- improving the explanation so the tested claim is easier to understand.

## How to raise a concern

Please open an issue and include:

1. the specific claim, measurement, or assumption you are questioning;
2. why it may be misleading or incomplete; and
3. a possible control, comparison, or revised measurement.

If you are commenting on an existing issue, please stay close to the experiment or evidence being discussed. Concrete negative results are welcome.

## Boundaries

- This project does not treat an agent's self-report as proof of consciousness or inner experience.
- Please do not submit private data, credentials, or proprietary implementation details.
- Please avoid claims about investment returns or guaranteed commercial outcomes.

## Before submitting code or documents

Please make sure that your contribution:

- describes what changed and why;
- separates observations from interpretations;
- does not expose private information or protected core IP; and
- keeps the language cautious when the evidence is limited.

At this early stage, a clear question or well-supported criticism may be more valuable than a large code change.
